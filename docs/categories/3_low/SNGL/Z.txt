Wiwidha jana kothas athara sahajeewanaya saha ekamuthukama wardhanaya kirema sadaha rajaya madihath wee prathisandhana wadasatahan kriyaathmaka kala yuthuya.
Palana paddhathiyaka pawathina durwalatha hadunagena ewa niwaradi kirema sadaha nithipatha prathiposhana laba ganeeme kramawedayak thibiya yuthuya.
Parisarika neethi ullanghanaya karana parshawanyanta erehiwa dadi piyawara ganeema magin swabhawika sampath araksha kara ganeemata hakki we.
Balaya pawathwa ganeema sadaha anugamanaya karanu labana upakramawalata wada rataye anagathaya wenuwen ganu labana theerana wagath kotasalakiya yuthuya.
Sama mattamakama palanaya saha kalamanakaranaya widyanthaka padanamaka pihita sidu kirema magin awinishchithathawayan magaharawa gatha hakkiya.
