
“PII risk is not just about entities 
it’s about how meaning is constructed in a sentence.”

d (proprietor) → the person (subject)
c (predicate) → the action/verb
r (remainder) → additional context (object, attributes, relationships)

In another words

the identity (e.g., public figure vs unknown person)
the action (e.g., “killed” vs “met”)
the context (e.g., “medical condition” vs “hobby”)

==========================

Same Keywords, Different Risk

“John was arrested for fraud”
“John read an article about fraud arrests”

this is why we need "action" to be considered instead of just flat context related key words
