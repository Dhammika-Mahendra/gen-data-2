Structure of the Information (How PII is expressed)

1. Atomic PII (APII) - About one person
2. Compound PII (CPII) - Involves multiple people        Example: “John is married to Mary”

Self vs Relational PII

Self-PII (SPII) → about the person directly      “John is depressed”
Relational PII → “John owns a house” ,  “John works with Mary”

=======================================================================================

Type of Information (Intrinsic Sensitivity)

Highly sensitive (intimate) - Medical, sexual, psychological data
Judgmental / harmful - Crimes, behavior, negative traits
Biographical / indirect - Name, job, location (lower risk alone)


=======================================================================================

Semantic Content (What is being said)  - Context/Meaning matters more than the entity itself

“John works” → low risk
“John committed fraud” → high risk
